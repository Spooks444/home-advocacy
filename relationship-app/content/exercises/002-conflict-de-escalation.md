# Conflict De-escalation

## Purpose
Lower emotional intensity during recurring conflict.

## Steps
- **Time-box**: 15 minutes maximum.
- **Topic limit**: One issue only.
- **Body scan**: Notice tension; if overwhelmed, request a 10-minute break.
- **Repair attempt**: Offer one specific request rather than criticism.
- **Close**: State one thing appreciated about the other person.

## Safety Rules
- Stop if either partner feels unsafe or unheard.
- No dredging past incidents.

## Usage
2-3 times per week when conflict repeats.
