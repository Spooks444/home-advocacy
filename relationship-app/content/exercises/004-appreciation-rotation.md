# Appreciation Rotation

## Purpose
Build positive sentiment and reduce defensiveness.

## Steps
- **Round 1**: Each partner names one specific thing they appreciate about the other.
- **Round 2**: Each partner names one thing the other did well recently.
- **Round 3**: Each partner states one thing they feel hopeful about.

## Safety Rules
- No backhanded compliments.
- No disqualifying the other’s statement.

## Usage
Once per week or after a difficult conversation.
