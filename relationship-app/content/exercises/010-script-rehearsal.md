# Script Rehearsal

## Purpose
Practice difficult conversations with safer wording.

## Steps
- **Draft**: Write a 3-sentence message using neutral wording.
- **Rehearse**: Read it aloud to yourself first.
- **Role-play**: Deliver the message to your partner; partner listens without rebuttal.
- **Refine**: Adjust one word together for clarity.

## Safety Rules
Do not escalate during rehearsal. Do not demand immediate resolution.

## Usage
Before a planned difficult conversation.
