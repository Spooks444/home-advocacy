# Empathy Loop

## Purpose
Practice hearing emotion before problem-solving.

## Steps
- **Speaker**: Share a recent situation and how it felt, no problem analysis.
- **Listener**: Repeat back the feeling, not the facts.
- **Swap**: Reverse roles with a new situation.
- **Settle**: Agree on one supportive phrase to use this week.

## Safety Rules
Do not fix while listening. Do not minimize feelings.

## Usage
Use during or after conflict.
