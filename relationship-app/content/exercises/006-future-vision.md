# Future Vision

## Purpose
Create shared short-term and long-term relationship goals.

## Steps
- **Individually**: Write 3 relationship goals for the next 30 days.
- **Share**: Read one goal each; partner reflects back meaning.
- **Combine**: Identify overlapping goals.
- **Commit**: Choose one joint goal and one metric to track.

## Safety Rules
No ridicule of goals. No pressure to disclose fears.

## Usage
Monthly planning session.
