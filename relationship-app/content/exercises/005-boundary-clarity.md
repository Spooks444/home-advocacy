# Boundary Clarity

## Purpose
Identify and communicate personal limits without conflict.

## Steps
- **Write**: Each partner writes 3 current boundaries privately.
- **Share**: One boundary each, without negotiation.
- **Acknowledge**: Partner repeats back what they heard; no rebuttal.
- **Agree**: Name one boundary both can honor this week.

## Safety Rules
Do not force disclosure. Pause if sharing feels unsafe.

## Usage
Monthly or when a boundary feels crossed.
