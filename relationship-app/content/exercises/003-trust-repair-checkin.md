# Trust Repair Check-in

## Purpose
Assess and rebuild safety after broken trust.

## Steps
- **Assessment**: Each partner rates current safety 1-5 privately.
- **Share**: Share rating and one reason without argument.
- **Request**: State one concrete behavior that would increase trust.
- **Boundary**: Name one boundary you need honored now.
- **Agreement**: If both want to continue, agree on one actionable step.

## Safety Rules
- Do not demand disclosure of private thoughts or records.
- Pause on shame or blame spirals.

## Usage
Once weekly during active repair, then as needed.
