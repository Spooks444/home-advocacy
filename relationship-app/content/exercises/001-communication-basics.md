# Communication Basics

## Purpose
Build safer, clearer communication patterns and reduce escalation during difficult topics.

## Structure
- **Check-in**: 60 seconds. Each partner states current mood in one word.
- **Prompt**: Use provided sentence stems. No rebuttals.
- **Reflection**: 2 minutes each. Partner listens without response, then switches.
- **Agreement**: Name one small behavioral change before ending.

## Safety Rules
- No name-calling, threats, or raised voices.
- Pause with the word "pause" if overwhelmed; resume only when both agree.
- Do not record sessions.

## Stems
- I feel ___ when ___ because ___.
- I need ___.
- I hear you saying ___.

## Usage
Use once per day. Do not combine with other exercises until comfortable with this format.
