# Trigger Map

## Purpose
Increase awareness of early warning signs and preferred support cues.

## Steps
- **Individually**: Write 5 triggers and 1 preferred support cue for each.
- **Share**: Exchange one trigger each; partner names one matching cue they can honor.
- **Post**: Keep cues visible or stored in a safe shared note.

## Safety Rules
No pressure to disclose. Pause if sharing feels unsafe.

## Usage
Monthly or after a high-conflict event.
