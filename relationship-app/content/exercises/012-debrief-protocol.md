# Debrief Protocol

## Purpose
Close sessions safely and reduce carryover tension.

## Steps
- **State**: Each partner gives a one-word status check.
- **Flag**: Name one thing to revisit later if needed.
- **Close**: Use a shared closing phrase or gesture.
- **Pause**: If tension is above 7/10, agree to pause and reconvene.

## Safety Rules
No new topics at closure. Do not force agreement.

## Usage
After every guided exercise or conflict session.
