# Repair Attempt

## Purpose
Learn the anatomy of a repair attempt and practice using it early.

## Steps
- **Identify**: Name the moment tension rises.
- **Attempt**: Use a pre-agreed repair phrase or gesture.
- **Accept**: Partner acknowledges the attempt without scoring it.
- **Return**: Resume the topic only if both agree.

## Safety Rules
Do not weaponize repair attempts. Do not demand repairs.

## Usage
Use weekly or during real-time conflict.
